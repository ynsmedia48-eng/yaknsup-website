The Best Kayaking Near Phoenix and Scottsdale Is at Canyon Lake

By Yak N Sup·June 2026·5 min read·Canyon Lake, AZ

If you're searching for kayaking or paddleboarding near Phoenix or Scottsdale, here's the honest answer: Canyon Lake Marina, 45 minutes east.

There are a few other options in the Phoenix metro — some urban lakes, the Salt River tubing corridor — but none of them compare to what you get at Canyon Lake. This is kayaking inside the Superstition Mountains, on a 950-acre reservoir with 500-foot canyon walls rising directly from the water. It's not a city park. It's the real thing.

Yak N Sup operates at Canyon Lake Marina on AZ-88, in the Tonto National Forest. We've been getting people on the water at Canyon Lake for years. No experience needed for any of our rentals — if you can sit in a chair, you can paddle a kayak.

What's Available

Solo

Single Kayak

The most popular rental. Stable, easy to paddle, and gives you full control of where you go. Great for individuals and groups who want their own boat.

Pair

Tandem Kayak

Two-person kayak — ideal for couples, parent/child pairs, or anyone who wants company in their boat. Both people paddle; the kayak is very stable.

Stand Up

Single SUP

Stand-up paddleboard for one. You can paddle standing or kneeling — beginners often start kneeling and stand up once they get comfortable. Great workout.

Stand Up · Pair

Tandem SUP

A wider, more stable paddleboard built for two. Unique experience — both people standing and paddling together. Popular with couples and adventurous pairs.

All rentals include paddle, life jacket (PFD), and a safety and paddling orientation from our dock crew. Dry bags are available to rent for $10.

Why Canyon Lake Over Other Options Near Phoenix

There are other places to paddle near Phoenix. Here's why Canyon Lake is different:

  • The setting. Canyon Lake is inside the Superstition Mountains, surrounded by canyon walls that rise 500–1,500 feet above the water. It looks nothing like a city park or a reservoir surrounded by suburbs. It looks like you drove into a wilderness.
  • Water quality. Canyon Lake is one of the clearest desert lakes in Arizona. The water is deep blue in the main channel, turquoise-green in the shallower coves. In calm sections you can see the bottom clearly.
  • Wildlife. Desert bighorn sheep on the canyon walls. Great blue herons in the creek mouths. Osprey, cormorants, and in winter, migratory ducks. You're in the Tonto National Forest — this is real wildlife habitat, not a manicured park.
  • The hidden coves. Canyon Lake has sections that are only accessible by paddle — La Barge Creek, Tortilla Creek, the sheltered no-wake zone at Boulder Recreation Area. These are places that boat traffic can't reach. You feel like you're discovering something.
  • No experience required. Our dock crew gets first-timers confident and comfortable before they leave the dock. We do this every day. It's not complicated.

Guided Canyon Tours

If you want more than a self-guided rental, our Guided Canyon Tours take you to the sections of the lake most people never find on their own — La Barge Creek, the hidden coves along the canyon wall, areas where bighorn sheep sightings are most reliable. Tours are 2 hours, led by one of our experienced guides, and available daily at 10:30 AM.

Guided tours are especially popular for first-timers who want orientation to the lake, visitors who want to maximize their time, and groups who want a structured experience rather than wandering on their own.

Special Events on the Water

Beyond standard rentals and tours, Yak N Sup offers monthly and seasonal special events:

  • Full Moon Paddle — offered monthly on the Saturday closest to the full moon. Evening departure, moonlight on the canyon walls and water. One of the most unique experiences available near Phoenix. Books out early.
  • Sunset Paddle — late afternoon launch timed to catch golden hour on the canyon walls. The light on orange rock reflected in still water is extraordinary.
  • Group events — bachelorette parties, corporate team building, birthday groups, family reunions. We handle groups of 6 to 20+ with advance booking.
✅ What to Know Before You Book

No experience needed. Life jackets included. Free parking at Canyon Lake Marina. No major stores on AZ-88 after Apache Junction — bring water, snacks, and sunscreen from home. Book online to guarantee your rental time slot, especially for weekend visits.
