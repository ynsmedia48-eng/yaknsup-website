About This Location

Texas on the Water

Hidden Cove is tucked into the shores of Lake Lewisville — a sprawling reservoir just north of Dallas with calm, protected coves that are perfect for beginners and wide open stretches that experienced paddlers love to explore.

The name says it all. It's the kind of spot locals know about and keep coming back to. Easy access, great parking, and a laid-back vibe that makes it one of the best places in North Texas to spend a day on the water.

Same great gear, same great guides, same Yak N Sup experience — just with more Texas in it.

Calm Coves

Protected waters perfect for first-timers and families with kids.

Open Water

Miles of open lake for those who want to explore and go farther.

Best Apr – Oct

Long Texas warm season. Open year-round weather permitting.

Near Dallas / DFW

Easy drive from Dallas, Fort Worth, and surrounding areas.

Come Prepared

What to Bring

We provide all the gear. Here's what to pack.

Sunscreen

Texas sun is intense on open water. SPF 50+ recommended.

Water

Bring plenty. Stay hydrated — especially in summer heat.

Water Shoes

Secure sandals or water shoes. No flip-flops on the water.

Sun Protection

Hat, sunglasses, and sun-protective clothing all help.

Quick-Dry Clothes

Something you don't mind getting wet. Board shorts work great.

Camera / Phone

Bring a waterproof case. The lake views are worth photographing.

Payment

Cash (use YNSCASH for discount) or card. Book online to skip the line.

Good Energy

Come ready to have fun. We'll take care of everything else.
