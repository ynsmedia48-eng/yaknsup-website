Good to Know

Rental Policies

We keep it simple. Here's what you need to know before you show up.

Reservations

  • Online booking recommended, especially on weekends
  • Walk-ups welcome based on availability
  • Arrive 15 minutes before your reserved start time
  • Text us at Canyon Lake — cell service is spotty

Cancellations

  • Full refund if cancelled 24+ hours in advance
  • Within 24 hours — reschedule for a future date
  • No-shows are non-refundable
  • Weather cancellations are always fully refunded

What to Bring

  • Clothes and shoes you're okay getting wet
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat
  • Water bottle (stay hydrated out there)
  • Leave flip-flops at home — they fall off

Age & Weight

  • Kids welcome — they ride with an adult in a tandem
  • All paddlers must wear a PFD on the water
  • Weight limits vary by craft — ask at check-in
  • Minors must have a waiver signed by a parent/guardian

Sign Your Waiver in Advance

Save time at check-in by completing your liability waiver online before you arrive. Takes about 2 minutes. Required for all participants.
