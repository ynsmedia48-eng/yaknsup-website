Kayaking & Paddleboarding at Lake Lewisville, Frisco Texas

Yak N Sup operates at Hidden Cove Marina on Lake Lewisville — located at 20400 Hackberry Creek Park Rd #200, Frisco, TX 75036. Hidden Cove is one of the most accessible and scenic paddling spots in the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex, with calm protected coves perfect for beginners and wide open water for those who want to explore.

We offer single kayak, tandem kayak, single SUP, and tandem SUP rentals. All craft types are priced at $35 for 2 hours, $65 for 4 hours, or $75 for a full day. No experience required — all rentals include a paddle, PFD, and safety briefing. We're open Thursday through Monday, 10am–8pm (9pm Friday and Saturday), and closed Tuesday and Wednesday.

Note: Hidden Cove Park charges a separate $20 vehicle entry fee. No glass is permitted at Lewisville Lake. Kids 8 and older can paddle independently. We do not offer refunds, but provide rain checks for cancellations due to rain within 24 hours or winds exceeding 18 mph.
